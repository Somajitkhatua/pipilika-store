The Craft

Every stitch tells a story

Pipli appliqué is a 900-year-old craft from the temple town of Pipili in Odisha. Artisans cut vivid cloth shapes — suns, lotuses, parrots and peacocks — and sew them onto vibrant backgrounds to create Chandua, the ceremonial canopy that adorns the chariots of Lord Jagannath.

We partner directly with 120+ artisan families, ensuring every piece is authentic, fair-traded, and made to order.
